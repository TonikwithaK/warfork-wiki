**Instagun** (IG) is a weapon with low rate of fire that shoots a single, instant kill beam of energy.

## Overview

This gun is exclusive to the [[Instagib]] gamemode, firing an instant killing beam of energy.

## Usage

Instagun is the only weapon present in instagib, requiring similar skills to the [[Electrobolt]]. Most tips for electrobolt should apply to instagun as well.

## Counters

Instagib is highly dependent on aiming skill. You can disrupt opponent’s aiming by moving unpredictably or breaking line of sight.

Take advantage of the opponents reload time, since the gamemode doesn't allow any other weapons it's a good time to line up a shot.

Warforkwiki.com is a wiki that can be edited by anyone. Players who wish to contribute and edit pages are encouraged to follow the guidelines listed here. All edits must also comply with wiki [[rules]].

## Editing guidelines

- Don't write your opinions. Only include facts.
- You're not allowed to use content from any other sources.
- Try to use gender-neutral phrases. It's generally better to use phrases like *player* instead of referring to the reader as *you*.
- Use headings to separate sections.
- Take a look at other wiki subpages. All pages should have a similar style.
- Use common sense.
- Consult with other members all major edits to any pages before submitting them.
- Make sure your edit won't break other pages. Renaming headers can cause other hyperlinks to stop working properly.
- Carefully review your edit before submitting it.

### Notes

- All content is and must be licensed according to [[Warfork:General disclaimer|general guidelines]].
- Protected pages cannot be edited.
- Contributions to Warfork's wiki may be edited, altered, or removed by other contributors. If you do not want your writing to be edited mercilessly, then do not submit it here.
- Administrators have a right to undo any edits without disclosing the reason.
- Warforkwiki is actively moderated. Users who submit low-effort edits may get punished and/or have their contributions undone.
- Only verified members are able to upload files. Ask an administrator if you want to upload a file.

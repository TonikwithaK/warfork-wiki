Rules define what is allowed and what is forbidden on Warforkwiki.com. Users must obey all the rules listed here. Breaking the rules may cause a user to get punished.
Additionally, all members must follow the [[Warfork:General disclaimer|general guidelines]].

## Rules

1.  **Respect everyone** - *Treat others how you want to be treated. Treat everyone equally.*
2.  **No harrassing** - *You may not harrass or bully any other member of the wiki..*
3.  **No sensitive content, topics** - *You may not upload any sensitive content like porn, gore, nudity. You may not talk about politics or religion.*
4.  **Original content only** - *Don't upload any copyrighted content.*
5.  **Fair use policy** - *Do not abuse editing / uploading tools. Do not promote any unofficial content.*
6.  **English only** - *All content must be in English.*
7.  **Direct links only** - *Link shorteners are prohibited due to viruses / privacy concerns.*

### Additional notes

- All content is and must be licensed according to [[Warfork:General disclaimer|general guidelines]].
- Administrators have a right to punish users for actions not mentioned in the rules above.
- Wiki rules can be changed anytime without the users being notified.

The Warfork IRC network can be accessed via the Warfork (in-game) by clicking Chat on the Main Menu or using an IRC client such as Colloquy, Hexchat, Irssi, KVirc, XChat, mIRC, etc.

You can connect to Warfork IRC by pointing your client at **irc.warfork.com** on port **6667** for plain text connections or ports **6697** for SSL encrypted connections.

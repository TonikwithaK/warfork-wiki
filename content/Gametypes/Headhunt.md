## Game type-exclusive information

Players get points for staying alive while being tagged. At the beginning of the game noone is tagged. Player who gets hit first becomes tagged. The goal of non-tagged players is to hunt down the tagged player. Player who eliminates the tagged player becomes tagged. The goal of a tagged player is to stay alive for as long as possible. Tagged player can defend himself by eliminating non-tagged players, which give a defender bonus of 5 points. They also receive 1 point per 2 seconds for staying alive. Each hit with a gunblade is rewarded with 1 point as a tagged player. Non-tagged players cannot eliminate each other however. Eliminating a non-tagged player while not being tagged punishes the player with -25 points.

### Tips

- Players should be careful while holding a gunblade if they're not tagged. Gunblade automatically attacks anyone in front of the player. This can result in unwanted frags and penalties for accidentally eliminating non-tagged players.

Bomb and Defuse is a unique team-based game type. Team Forbidden is the attacking team - their objective is to plant the bomb and protect it until it explodes or eliminate the enemy team. The objective of team ICY is to protect bombsites, defuse the bomb (if planted) or eliminate the enemy team. Points are given for completing the objectives. Players get to choose which weapons they spawn with. At the beginning of the round, one of the players in team Forbidden is randomly chosen as a bomb carrier.

The bomb can be dropped by using the "drop flag" command (which can be bound to a specific key).
The bomb will be automatically placed when the bomb carrier is on the site. After the bomb is placed, it must be armed. Arming and defusing the bomb simply requires a player to be near the bomb for a short amount of time.
The bomb cannot be moved once it is placed down.

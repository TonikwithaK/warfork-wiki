Such information may be collected through forms when You register, use web-based software tools, license downloadable software, provide feedback through an online survey, sign up for e-mail notifications, or complete other forms that may appear on this Site.

This type of information includes among other things technical information, such as Your IP address and Web browser type. We also log and track which pages are visited to enable us to measure traffic usage and patterns.

**Cookies:** Like many sites, this Site uses "cookies" to recognize Your computer on subsequent visits to this Site. Cookies are small files created by Web sites that reside on the hard drive of Your computer and that store information about Your use of a particular Web site. This Site uses cookies to store preferences, and other data pertinent to Your use of the Site to make Your use of the Site more productive. This Site will not operate correctly if cookies are not accepted by Your computer. The cookies from this Site are required for access and participation in the Site. The cookies placed on Your computer from the Site are intended to be used solely on the computer You use at the time of the cookie's creation. You may not copy, modify or alter such cookies in any way, except as changed through the normal use of the Site. The help file of an Internet browser application (for example, Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox) contains information about rejecting and explicitly managing cookies on a computer.
